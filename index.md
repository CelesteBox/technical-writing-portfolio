### Welcome


I design structured technical documentation focused on clarity, evaluation systems, and workflow definition. My work centers on translating complex processes into usable, consistent documentation that can function as a source of truth across teams.

This portfolio includes technical writing samples focused on software, SaaS B2B products, APIs, developer-facing documentation, and applied AI.

The work emphasizes practical documentation formats such as workflows, evaluation systems, and structured technical explanations, with a focus on clarity, usability, and real-world engineering contexts.

The articles in this portfolio are written in English, Spanish, and Portuguese (Brazil). Each piece reflects the same documentation standards, regardless of language.


### About me

I am a technical writer with a background in systems thinking, applied ethics, and education. This combination allows me to anticipate reader friction and structure documentation with clarity and intent.

My work focuses on translating complex technical concepts into actionable documentation for developers, product teams, and technical stakeholders. I approach technical writing as translation with purpose: ensuring that understanding leads to execution.

I am particularly interested in documentation as a bridge between engineering, product, and decision-making processes.
